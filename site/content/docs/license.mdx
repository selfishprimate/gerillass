## What that means in practice

You may use Gerillass in commercial and private work, modify it, and
redistribute it. Two conditions come with that: keep the copyright notice and
the licence text with any copy of the source you pass on, and state what you
changed if you distribute a modified version.

The licence also grants patent rights from the contributors, which is the part
that separates Apache 2.0 from MIT, and it offers no warranty.

None of this reaches the CSS you compile. What the library gives you is Sass
that runs at build time; the stylesheet that comes out the other side is yours.
